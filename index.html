Move like Squirtle

Move through the frontier as Squirtle. Dive to the centre of the Lab basin to enter the Deep Record; stand still inside a band of strata to log it. Tap Water Jet for a movement burst; hold it for a Water Hose that suppresses fire and pushes on some physical obstacles. There is no quest checklist: follow slopes, shorelines, animals, other Squirtles, weather, and whatever changes around you.
